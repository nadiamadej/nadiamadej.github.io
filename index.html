Wonders of the World

Through a meditative practise of entangling the images together, the author explores the connection between humans and nature. The outcomes are woven collages put together in the order of the original magazine pages, showing the front (A) and back (B) of the individual page.

This publication contains collages made by using images from the National Geographic magazine titled Wonders of the World, printed in 2018.

A Catalogue for an online Antique Bookshop

A visual system and a printed publication containing around a hundred books and/or printed matter. The catalogue presenting a selection of books for the new season designed to be sent to potential buyers in the season of Spring/Summer collection 2026.

Designed sections also showcase custom made glyphs and colour coded columns presenting additional details/specs. printed and online version May 2026

The online Catalogue available at: https://historiamundi.pl/pl/content/32-katalog-wiosna-2026

From Hand to hand

From hand to hand explores how graphic design can be used as a tool to synthesize singular stories into a collective base of knowledge and a comprehensive visual archive. The publication reimagines Femmage (feminine+collage) in the context of graphic design, by developing a visual language that weaves content ranging from feminist magazines and archival images into bigger narratives. Amongst the main contents of the book, there are the author's original entries—footage from a series of embroidery workshops organised for the students of the KABK. A coded website presenting my bachelor thesis in an online environment is available to view at: https://kabk.github.io/go-theses-25-nadia-madej/
